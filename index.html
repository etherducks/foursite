Overview

Each question is a Uniswap v4 pool holding two shares that are worth exactly one dollar between them. Which one holds all of it isn't decided until the question closes.

Pricing

A share worth 67 cents is the market saying: roughly two in three, as far as anyone here is willing to bet. Buy it and that number moves. It was never a claim of fact, only of appetite.

Resolution

A named reporter posts what actually happened. FoursiteHook reads that report, freezes the pool, and lets each side redeem at the value it was owed all along: one dollar, or nothing.

If the reporter never posts, or the outcome can't be pinned down, both sides settle evenly instead of guessing on your behalf.
